# 🤖 Askie – The No-Filter AI Chatbot

> *"I'm not here to make friends, I'm here to answer your questions... poorly."*

Askie is an AI chatbot with attitude. It doesn’t sugarcoat, doesn’t pretend, and doesn’t really care what you think. But hey — at least it gives you a response. Blunt, sarcastic, maybe even rude... but always honest(ish).

---

## 🧠 About

Askie was built as a fun twist on the typical friendly chatbot. It mimics a human-like personality with zero professionalism — making it ideal for entertainment, testing, or roasting your friends.
